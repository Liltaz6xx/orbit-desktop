ORBIT Desktop runs in your Windows account. Setup downloads Python, the
required libraries, Ollama, the Qwen2.5 1.5B language model and the Kokoro
voice files as needed. A fresh installation downloads about 3 GB. Make sure
you have an Internet connection and several GB of free disk space.
Updating from 0.7.0-beta.6 downloads the faster Kokoro voice model (326 MB).

While ORBIT is open, it advertises "Playing ORBIT" to the Discord desktop
client if it is running. Discord's activity-sharing settings control
visibility. Your conversations and saved memories stay local.

To update ORBIT Desktop, download and run the newer Setup EXE. Close ORBIT
when Setup asks. Your saved memories and settings remain in your local profile.
